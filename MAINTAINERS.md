## Triage

Issues are triaged on a best-effort basis. Active maintainers aim to:

- Acknowledge new issues within 5 business days.
- Apply at least one label (`bug`, `enhancement`, `question`, `dependencies`,
  `good first issue`, `help wanted`).
- Close issues that are duplicates, off-topic, or have not received a reply
  for 60 days, with a short explanation.

## Reviews

PRs require **at least one approving review from an active maintainer** before
merging. Maintainers may self-merge trivial fixes (typos, dependency bumps,
CI tweaks) but should still post a brief rationale on the PR.

## Releases

Only active maintainers can trigger the `Release` workflow that pushes to NuGet.
The release process is documented in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#releasing).

## Becoming a maintainer

We welcome new maintainers. Reasonable signals that someone is ready:

- Several merged PRs touching different parts of the codebase.
- A track record of helpful, thoughtful issue triage / PR review.
- Familiarity with the architecture (see [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)).

If that describes you, open an issue titled `Maintainer nomination: <your handle>`
or reach out to an active maintainer privately.
