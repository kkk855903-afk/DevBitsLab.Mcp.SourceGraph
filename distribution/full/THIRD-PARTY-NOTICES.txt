SourceGraph MCP Full Offline Bundle
===================================

This bundle redistributes the following third-party model:

  Name: jina-embeddings-v2-base-code
  Author: Jina AI
  Source: https://huggingface.co/jinaai/jina-embeddings-v2-base-code
  License: Apache License 2.0
  Included files: model.onnx, tokenizer.json

The model is used locally to generate code-search embeddings. It is not a
conversational or generative language model. The full Apache License 2.0 text
is included beside this notice as APACHE-2.0.txt.
